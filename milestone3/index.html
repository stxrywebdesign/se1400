Big Brother: Slides Edition is a fan-created reality competition that aired via Google Slides in 2023. The show follows 16 fictional contestants competing in traditional Big Brother format with HOH competitions, nominations, Power of Veto, and evictions.

The series went viral on TikTok and Twitter, gaining 2.4M+ views and inspiring similar digital reality shows. The finale episode reached 425k views.
